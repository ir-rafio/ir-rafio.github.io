The idea hit me immediately after watching the conversation between Desmond and Twilight. Desmond was talking very calmly to Twilight, but his deep breathing and blank face suggested he was not in the best mood. At first, it seemed like a wild thought, but after watching the scene again, I feel there's good reason to believe it.

- Anya went to sleep before meeting Desmond, taking away the chance to falsify this theory.
- Desmond stays away from people despite being involved in politics. It could be a case like Mao from Code Geass.
- Despite usually staying away from people, he talked to Loid without hesitation. Maybe he wanted to know more about Twilight.
- The scars on his head could be a result of experiments.
