During the Covid lockdown, my friend Jubayer recommended Gurren Lagann to me, just because it randomly popped up in front of him. He hadn't watched it himself, and none of us had any idea what it was about. I started watching it only because I thought the name was funny.

While watching, I noted down my reaction after every episode, so I could show Jubayer how his random recommendation was turning out. For the first few episodes, it was nothing special. But after episode 8, it turned out to be an absolute gem.

I will never forget how I discovered this anime, and how I went in with zero expectations and ended up experiencing this peak!
