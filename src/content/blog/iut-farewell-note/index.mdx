As salamu alaikum, everyone.

I'm still having trouble processing that it's all over. Although I've been trying to mentally prepare for this for almost a year.

It's been quite a period of 4 and a half years for me. I'm not sure how much ready I am for the next phase. But pray for me and please forgive me if you have any bad memories with me.

As I've just graduated, I'm most probably not very qualified to give you advices. But I guess I can share some perspectives:

1. The environment and people of IUT are unlike any other. This is the greatest resource of IUT. If you can't take advantage of this, you're wasting your money. Remember that cooperation \> competition.
2. It's way better to be pro-active than reactive. Understanding the flaws in the system is good, but you need to spend most of your energy to focus on what IS in your control and do the best you can from your situation.
3. Invisible achievements are more valuable than visible ones in the long run. Try to be 'more solid and less shiny'.
4. Don't become a slave to materialism. Friendships, memories, joy, sadness and other intangible assets are more important than meets the eye. HYPER PRODUCTIVITY IS FOR LOSERS.
5. Many aspects of life are counterintuitive. If you try to maximize happiness, you'll be disappointed. If you're focused on 'creating memories', you can't enjoy the moment. And you cannot achieve proper maturity without finding the time to be immature.
6. Take your important decisions cautiously. But if you find yourself stuck in 'analysis paralysis', just take a leap of faith.
7. Don't try to optimize everything. Embrace a bit of chaos and randomness. Where's the fun in life if it can't surprise you?
8. Always remember your identity. Never change yourself just to 'fit in'.
9. Whatever you do, NEVER FORGET TO HAVE FUN!

10. This last one is a request rather than a suggestion. IUT has some good reputation (There is no thief in IUT, IUT is always clean, ...) - try to preserve them. And please try to make sure that the 'I' in IUT has a meaning.

If you ever need any help, feel free to knock me even if we've never met before. I'm not saying this as a formality. I would really be grateful for any opportunity to be useful.

I love you and will miss you a lot!

Ladies & gentlemen, Rafio (190041125) has left the building!
