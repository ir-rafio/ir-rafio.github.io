I will not make what the customers want. I will make what I want and wait for my customers to find me. Otherwise, where will my customers go?

The paragraph above is a part of my long-standing ethos, specially in terms of teaching. Some of my beliefs turn out to be unpopular. That makes me a man who is intentionally walking a difficult path.

This is why appreciations like these are very dear to me. It gives me the reassurance that I am doing something right and helps me remain firm in the things I believe are important. More than anything, moments like these remind me to be grateful to Allah for giving me the opportunity to be a teacher.
